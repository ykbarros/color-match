    How to play

    1. Memorize. A color fills the screen for 5 seconds (2 on Hard).
    2. Recreate. Rebuild it from memory with the hue, saturation and brightness sliders.
    3. Score. Lock it in to see how close you got, out of 10. Closeness is measured the way eyes see it (CIEDE2000), not by raw numbers.

    Five colors per game, 50 points max. The daily has the same five colors for everyone. Practice deals new ones every time, and you can send a friend your exact colors from the results screen.
